## The main thing

**Two mutations left the suite entirely green.** Both were not in the code but in checks that
were guarding something other than what they appeared to guard.

This is the third stage in a row where an independent review finds a check with the right
verdict and too weak an assertion. The form differs every time, the substance is the same.

## What went from this into PLAYBOOK

Five lessons (§5). Two are worth a separate mention.

**A check that guards a constant must not iterate over that same constant.** The "the list is
empty" mutation walks straight through the check that exists precisely against it.

**"NOT EVALUATED" has to be a state of its own.** Otherwise a green suite means less than it
appears to — and no amount of discipline compensates, because the difference is not visible in
the output.

And a third, about the tooling itself: the mutation harness was moved out into
`scripts/mutate.py`, because three lines done in place let us down three times — with a stale
`.pyc` after a rollback within the same second, with a killed run that left the file broken, and
with a measurement on code that did not compile. Silently, every time.
