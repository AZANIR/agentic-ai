## The main finding: a check that cannot be made red

`check_another_owners_facts_never_reach_the_context` asserted the right thing and **could never
fail**. The fixture stored somebody else's fact with the text «Доставляти на Банков**у** 11»,
while both assertions looked for the substring «Банков**а**». The nominative form does not occur
in the text — Ukrainian declines the street name — so `not any(...)` was true every time,
including with a memory that had **no owner filter at all**.

This is the third occurrence of the same trap in the course («Київ»/«Києві» at stage 1,
«Володимирська»/«Володимирську» right here). The first two made a check **red** and were
therefore found within minutes. This one made it **green forever** — and only the direct question
"what exactly would have to break for this to fail" found it.

Fixed by reconciling on the stem (`Банков`) — and, separately, by an assertion **before** both
negations: first prove that the fixture is capable of producing a match at all.

## Checks with no teeth

Six checks had the right verdict and too weak an assertion:

- **AC-01b** asserted that the summary **survived**. An implementation that feeds the summary
  into compression a second time and appends the result left it green. The assertion is now about
  the **input** to compression.
- **AC-06c** took a hostile fact that had nothing in common with the question's words — so it did
  not clear the threshold, and "the text does not change the order" was proved on a fact that is
  not in the context.
- **AC-03** did not check the limit at all: the mutation removing `len(taken) < limit` gave
  **zero** reds. `Context` now carries `limit` alongside `threshold`.
- **AC-09** had no assertion about the fake's banner or about being offline — both exist at
  stages 2 and 3.
- **AC-07** compared only the implementations' names and the length of the list.
- **AC-01** for scene 1 of the demo asserted the heading instead of the body — exactly what the
  comment next to it promises not to do.
- **The clock guard** saw only calls through the attribute: `from time import time` walked past
  it, and it looked only at `facts.py`.

## Prose that had drifted from the code

- **NFR-4** said "≤ 5 s", while the ceiling was held by the constant `BUDGET_SECONDS = 30`. The
  number in the document held nothing — the same class of defect stage 4 declared closed two days
  earlier.
- **SAD §8** and the C4 diagram promised a trace with the reason for every rejection. No such
  trace exists: `long_term` does not import `shared.trace` at all. §8 was brought into line with
  what is there, with a note that the difference becomes a task for stage 8.
- **The lesson** promised that semantic retrieval overcomes the synonym boundary. On the default
  `hash-words` it gives **the same 0.00** — that one is word-by-word too. The promise was replaced
  with the truth: it shows that **the scales differ**, and that real synonyms need
  `fastembed`/`openai`.
- **`decision.py`** held a `BUDGET_SECONDS` that nobody reads, with a comment about `check_all`.
- **`__init__.py`** said "Status: not started (skeleton)" on a finished stage.
- **`exercises.md`** claimed that both isolation checks see somebody else's fact.

## What held up

Verified by the reviewers independently, with runs:

- **ADR-0004 is honoured**: the owner filter sits before the selection, and the "after the
  selection" mutation goes red from precisely the mirror check.
- **ADR-0001, ADR-0002, ADR-0003 are honoured** — the JSONL file, contradiction by topic, expiry
  at retrieval; time is a keyword parameter everywhere.
- **The AC-06b fixture leans on the scores** (0.75 against 0.50) rather than on the stability of
  `sorted()`.
- **The reconciliation of `DECISION.md` against `RULES`** runs in three independent directions and
  found a divergence on the very first run.
- **The conventions are followed literally**: no `if profile ==`, no `openai.OpenAI()`, 27 checks
  out of 42 on failure modes, the whole suite offline with no key.
- **The numbers in the prose are computed**: the sizes of all six modules, the number of checks and
  all eleven red counts in the exercises are reconciled automatically.

## Summary

Twenty-seven findings on a stage its author considered finished. The distribution is instructive:
**eleven** are real defects in the code, **seven** are checks with no teeth, **six** are prose that
had drifted from the code, **three** are deferred with an owner and a deadline.

The most valuable finding cost not a single line of code: the question "what exactly would have to
break for this check to go red" found four checks whose answer was "nothing".
