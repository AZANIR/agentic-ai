# T6 — Health and metrics: every dependency's health on its own

- **Layer:** `ports`
- **Deps:** T1, T2
- **Criteria:** [AC-06](../spec.md), [AC-06b](../spec.md), [AC-06c](../spec.md), [AC-11](../spec.md)
- **Touches:** `stages/s06_platform/observe.py`

## Why

**A positive verdict is a criterion of its own.** An endpoint hard-wired to "broken" satisfies
AC-06 and AC-11 together. A monitor that always screams is the same defect as a guard that lets
nobody through.

Metrics are process-local state too, and that is the third face of the cause from ADR-0002. The
reconciliation against the number of traces is asserted for one worker, and the lesson says so
outright.

## Definition of Done

Health names every dependency separately and is reachable with no key; metrics are closed behind a key and tell the kinds of failure apart; a broken dependency gives a named error rather than a crash
